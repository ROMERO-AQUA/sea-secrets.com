Frequently Asked Questions

All full-day trips include hotel pick-up and drop-off, snorkeling equipment, an experienced guide, and a fresh open buffet lunch served on board. Water and soft drinks are also included.

No prior experience is required. Our certified instructors provide a full safety briefing and can guide complete beginners. Non-divers are also welcome and can snorkel at the surface while certified divers explore below.

All group trips depart at 9:00 AM. Our team will collect you from your hotel lobby. Please be ready 15 minutes before departure. The private speedboat can be arranged flexibly between 9 AM and 2 PM.

Yes, children are welcome on all island and dolphin trips. We recommend ages 5+ for snorkeling. The deep sea diving trip has a minimum age of 10 for non-diving participants. Please inform us when booking.

Sunscreen (reef-safe preferred), a hat, sunglasses, a towel, swimwear, and a change of clothes. Snorkeling gear is provided. For diving, bring a valid diving certification card if you have one.

Booking is done via WhatsApp with our team at +20 111 089 0202. Payment is typically made on the day of the trip. Contact us to confirm availability and discuss payment options.
